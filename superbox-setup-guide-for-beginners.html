SuperBox Setup Guide for Beginners

A simple beginner setup walkthrough for plugging in, connecting, and adjusting a SuperBox.

What you need

You need the SuperBox, power adapter, HDMI cable, remote, batteries if required, a TV with HDMI, and internet through Wi-Fi or Ethernet.

Step 1: connect the hardware

Plug the HDMI cable into the box and TV. Plug in power. Turn on the TV and select the correct HDMI input.

Step 2: connect internet

Choose Ethernet for the most stable setup or connect to Wi-Fi through settings. Enter the Wi-Fi password carefully and confirm the connection.

Step 3: check display and time settings

Adjust screen size, resolution, time zone, and language if needed. Correct time settings can matter for apps and guide features.

Step 4: learn the remote

Test home, back, arrows, OK/select, volume, voice command, and app shortcuts. A backlit remote can be helpful in dark rooms.

Step 5: save support information

Keep your order number, seller contact, warranty terms, and certificate or authorization information if provided. Future-you will be happy you did.
