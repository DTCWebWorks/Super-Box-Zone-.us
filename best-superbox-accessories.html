Accessories

Best SuperBox Accessories: Ethernet Cable, HDMI, Remote, and Setup Gear

Simple accessories that can make a SuperBox setup cleaner, faster, and easier to use.

Ethernet cable

The most useful accessory may be a simple Ethernet cable. If your router is close enough, wired internet can reduce buffering and improve reliability.

Quality HDMI cable

Most people do not need a luxury HDMI cable, but they do need one that works reliably and fits the TV setup without pulling loose.

Keyboard remote

A mini keyboard remote can make typing passwords, searches, and app names much easier than using arrow buttons.

Surge protector

A small surge protector helps protect the TV, box, router, and other entertainment devices from power spikes.

Cable management

Velcro straps, short HDMI cables, and clean placement can reduce clutter and help the remote signal work better.

Bottom line

Accessories do not need to be expensive. The best ones solve real friction: connection, control, power, and clean placement.
