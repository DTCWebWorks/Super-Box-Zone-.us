Buyer Safety

How to Tell if a SuperBox Seller Is Legit

A practical checklist for avoiding sketchy sellers and looking for authorization proof.

Start with seller transparency

A legit seller should make it easy to find contact details, warranty terms, return policy, shipping information, and support instructions.

Look for certificate or authorization proof

Some reseller systems use certificates, authorization codes, or verification pages. If a seller claims to be authorized, ask where you can verify that claim.

Be careful with suspicious discounts

A price that is dramatically lower than the normal market can be a warning sign. Cheap is not helpful if support, activation, warranty, or authenticity becomes a problem.

Check return policy before buying

Do not wait until something goes wrong. Read the return window, restocking rules, and who pays shipping.

Keep proof

Save screenshots, receipts, order numbers, seller pages, and warranty text. If the listing changes later, your records matter.

Bottom line

The box matters, but the seller matters too. Buy from a source that gives you proof, support, and clear policies.
